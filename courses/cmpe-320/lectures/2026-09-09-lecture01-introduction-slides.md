## Grading scheme — matches the syllabus

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Four assignments | 30% |
| One project | 35% |
| One final exam | 35% |

Assignments are **due roughly every three weeks starting week 3**. Electronic submission through
OnQ, **one submission per person**. Use **ISO C++**; any IDE is fine for the GUI.

## Assignment rules

- Marks and comments are uploaded to OnQ by your TA.
- Each assignment has **one week** as the deadline plus an **Emergency Period of one week**.
- That 7-day extension is **given to all students**, including those with accommodations and
  short-term considerations.

### "Group efforts" — the academic integrity line

- You **are** encouraged to discuss difficulties with peers and your TA.
- You **may** review and learn from other people's code, to explore alternatives or get help in a
  particular area.
- You **may not copy** other people's code.

## C++ team project

- Details on OnQ. You are assigned to **agile teams by the Head TA**.
- **Each team member must write code and participate fairly.**
- Groups are formed during the **first tutorial**.

## Email subject format

> ⚠️ **The slide contradicts itself.** It says the subject has "3 parts separated by underscore
> `_`" and "insert `_` between the three parts", but the example on the same slide is written with
> hyphens: `320-1234-Topic`. The syllabus is unambiguous and uses **underscores**:
>
> ```
> 320_<last four digits of student ID>_<topic>
> ```
>
> Use the underscore form.

## Final exams

Don't finalise travel until the exam schedule is posted; exams are not moved for travel or flights,
and under Academic Regulation 8.3 all on-campus finals are written on the Kingston campus.
