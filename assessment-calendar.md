Every deliverable that carries marks, across all six courses, in date order.
Built 2026-09-14 from the filed syllabi in `courses/*/references/`. **Those syllabi remain the
authority** — if this file and a syllabus disagree, the syllabus wins and this file is stale.

> **Term:** 8 September – 8 December 2026 · **Fall break:** 12–18 October (no classes)
> **Final exam period:** 10–23 December 2026

## TLDR

**Three weeks carry almost everything.** The week of **5 October** has four graded assessments plus
an assignment deadline. The week of **9 November** has four more. **1 December** is a CMPE 365 test
*and* an ENPH 334 quiz on the same day. Everything else is comparatively spread out.

**The single heaviest item before December is CMPE 365 Test 1 on 24 September** — 20% of that
course, two weeks in.

**Nothing is worth more than the finals**, which are 35–65% of five of the six courses and are all
still unscheduled. CMPE 365 has no final at all.

## Pressure points

**Week of 5 October — five graded items in five days.**
Tue evening MTHE 326 Midterm 1 (20%) → Wed ELEC 371 quiz (25%) *and* CMPE 320 A2 due → Thu CMPE 365
Test 2 (20%) → Fri MTHE 351 Quiz 1 (20%). That is **85% of marks-weight across four courses in one
week**, and the ELEC 371 quiz alone is a quarter of that course. Start CMPE 320 A2 early — it is the
only one of the five you can move.

**Week of 9 November — four in four days.**
Tue ENPH 334 Quiz 3 → Wed CMPE 320 A3 → Thu CMPE 365 Test 4 (20%) → Fri CMPE 320 SDD. Two CMPE 320
deliverables in the same week as a 20% test.

**Tuesday 1 December — two on one day.**
ENPH 334 Quiz 4 in the 11:30 tutorial, CMPE 365 Test 5 in the 13:30 lecture. **They do not overlap**
(11:30–12:30 vs 13:30–14:20) but leave no gap for last-minute revision between them.

**6 Oct and 18 Nov are evening sittings.** Both MTHE 326 midterms run 18:00–19:30 on days unrelated
to the lecture schedule. They will not announce themselves in a lecture slot.

### All-or-nothing and no-make-up rules worth knowing

- **ENPH 334 labs are all-or-nothing.** If all labs are not completed satisfactorily, **the entire
  39% lab section is zeroed** — regardless of how the individual pieces scored. Attendance is
  mandatory and recorded, in your registered section only.
- **ELEC 371: sitting in the wrong assigned quiz room = automatic 20% penalty** on the quiz. Room
  assignments arrive by email beforehand.
- **CMPE 365 has no make-up tests** and no final to absorb a missed one.
- **MTHE 326 quizzes have no accommodation** beyond the four dropped scores — no make-ups, no
  documentation accepted.
- **MTHE 351: a missed quiz with a valid reason moves its weight to the final** — no make-up sitting.
- **ELEC 371: a missed quiz with an approved absence moves its 25% to the final.** File the Smith
  Engineering form; **do not email the instructor.**
- **CMPE 320 assignments have a one-week deadline plus an automatic second week** for emergencies
  only — explicitly *not* a two-week deadline.

## Still unknown — four gaps

These are not in any filed syllabus. Everything above is; these are not.

1. **All five final exam dates.** Scheduled centrally (Arts & Science / the Exams Office / the
   Registrar) and published on SOLUS. MTHE 326's outline gives the window as **10–23 December**;
   the others just say "the exam period". **Do not book travel until the timetable posts.**
2. **MTHE 351 homeworks 2–10.** Only Homework 1 (21 Sep) has a stated date. Ten homeworks across
   twelve weeks implies roughly weekly, but the cadence is not written down — they appear on
   Crowdmark/OnQ.
3. **ELEC 371's four lab dates.** The syllabus deliberately gives none: "consult your individual
   SOLUS timetable, and note the lab-session dates at the top of the tables." Worth transcribing
   from SOLUS into this file once you have them, since labs there are attendance-gated with no
   credit for the wrong session.
4. **CMPE 320's project 35% breakdown.** RAD (30 Oct), SDD (13 Nov) and the presentation (week of
   30 Nov) are dated, but how the 35% splits between them — and whether there are further team
   deliverables — is not in the syllabus. It is on OnQ, which is where the live schedule lives.

Smaller unknowns, unknowable by design: **MTHE 326's 12 quizzes are unannounced** (pop quizzes at
the end of lectures and tutorials, doubling as attendance), and **ENPH 334's design-project due
dates are given by week, not by day** — the exact day is your own lab session within weeks 8 and 11.
